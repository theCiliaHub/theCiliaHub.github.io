Welcome to CiliaHub 🧬

CiliaHub is the central repository for the **CiliAI** project, dedicated to collecting, curating, and sharing comprehensive data related to ciliary and flagellar biology. Our goal is to provide researchers with a unified platform for exploring the **ciliome** across various species and functional contexts.

Key Resources & Datasets

The data driving the CiliAI Explorer is sourced and maintained directly through CiliaHub:

  • **Master Gene Database:** Over 23,000 genes with detailed functional annotations, orthologs, and human disease links (Ciliopathies, OMIM).
  • **Phylogenetic Matrices:** Conservation matrices based on seminal studies (Li et al., 2014; Nevers et al., 2017) to study gene evolution across taxa.
  • **High-Content Screen Data:** Consolidated results from large-scale CRISPR and RNAi screens detailing effects on ciliation and ciliary length.
  • **Single-Cell RNA-seq (scRNA-seq):** Tissue-specific expression data to localize gene activity to specific cell types (e.g., lung ciliated cells).

Our Mission

We aim to foster collaboration and accelerate discovery in ciliopathy research by making complex genomic and functional datasets easily accessible and searchable through the CiliAI conversational interface. We welcome contributions and feedback to improve our database accuracy and coverage.
