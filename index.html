FOXJ1 Overview

Forkhead box J1 - Master regulator of motile ciliogenesis. Essential for the development and function of motile cilia in respiratory epithelia, brain ventricles, and reproductive tracts.

Transcription Factor Ciliogenesis High Expression

Expression Pattern

Highly expressed in ciliated cells across multiple tissues. Shows strong enrichment in respiratory epithelium and ependymal cells.

Key Functions

• Regulates motile cilia formation
• Controls basal body positioning
• Activates ciliary gene programs
• Essential for mucociliary clearance
