About CiliAI

I am an **AI assistant** designed to help you explore and analyze data on **ciliary biology**. You can ask questions or paste full gene lists.

Data Sources

My data comes from a pre-compiled database of over **23,000 genes**, enriched with **8 specialized datasets** (CORUM, UMAP, scRNA, Phylogeny, etc.). This comprehensive integration allows for deep contextual analysis of ciliary genes.

Capabilities

CiliAI is built to understand natural language queries related to genetics, protein function, localization, and disease. When you paste a list of genes, CiliAI automatically annotates and classifies the list based on the underlying datasets.

**Examples of what I understand:**
  • **Gene Details:** "What is IFT88?" or "Describe ARL13B."
  • **Localization:** "List genes in the transition zone." or "Where is CEP290?"
  • **Complexes:** "What genes are in the BBSome?" or "Complex components of OFD1."
  • **Ciliopathies:** "Gene list of Joubert Syndrome." or "What genes cause PCD?"
  • **Phylogeny:** "Show conservation of IFT88."
  • **Domains:** "Which genes have WD40 domains?"
